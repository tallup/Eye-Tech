- **Hover Effects** - Interactive table rows
- **Responsive** - Scrollable on mobile devices

#### **4. Transaction Summary:**
- **Large Display** - Easy-to-read financial summary
- **Three Columns** - Subtotal, Discount, Total Amount
- **Color-Coded** - Different colors for different amounts
- **Bold Typography** - Important numbers stand out

#### **5. Notes Section:**
- **Conditional Display** - Only shows if notes exist

- **Items Table** - All products with pricing details
- **Summary** - Financial totals and calculations
- **Notes** - Additional information if available

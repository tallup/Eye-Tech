## 📊 **System Overview:**

### **🏗️ Database Structure:**

#### **1. Sales Table:**
- ✅ **Sale Number** - Auto-generated unique identifier (SALE-YYYYMMDD-XXXXXX)
- ✅ **Customer Information** - Name, phone, email
- ✅ **Payment Details** - Method (cash, card, mobile_money, bank_transfer)
- ✅ **Transaction Status** - Pending, Completed, Cancelled, Refunded
- ✅ **Financial Tracking** - Subtotal, tax amount, discount amount, total amount
- ✅ **User Tracking** - Which staff member made the sale
- ✅ **Notes** - Additional transaction details

#### **2. Sales Items Table:**
- ✅ **Product Details** - Name, SKU, description from product catalog
- ✅ **Quantity & Pricing** - Quantity sold, unit price, discounts
- ✅ **Automatic Calculations** - Total price calculated automatically
- ✅ **Product Relationships** - Links to product catalog for inventory updates

## 🚀 **How to Use:**

### **📱 Creating a New Sale:**
1. Go to **Admin Panel** → **Sales**
2. Click **"Create Sale"**
3. Fill in customer information
4. Select payment method and status
5. Enter financial details (subtotal, tax, discount, total)
6. Add notes if needed
7. Save the transaction

### **📊 Viewing Sales:**
1. Go to **Admin Panel** → **Sales**
2. View all transactions in organized table
3. Use filters to find specific sales
4. Search by customer name or sale number
5. Click on any sale to view/edit details

### **🔍 Filtering & Search:**
- **Status Filter:** Show only pending, completed, or cancelled sales
- **Payment Filter:** Show only cash, card, mobile money, or bank transfer sales
- **Search:** Find sales by customer name, phone, or sale number
- **Sort:** Sort by date, amount, or customer name
